Campus Monitoring Deployment

{{ latitude }}° N, {{ longitude }}° E

Station deployed at NMIT Campus, Yelahanka, Bengaluru. Telemetry is streamed from an ESP32 microcontroller over Wi-Fi to ThingSpeak cloud storage and consolidated in this Flask framework.

Microcontroller: ESP32 DevKit V1 (Tensilica Xtensa Dual-Core)

Viva Defense Key Technical Insights

  • Autoregressive Trend Continuity: For short horizons (h=1h), Linear Regression achieved MAE of 4.11 and R² of 0.750, matching or slightly outperforming persistence. Non-linear tree ensembles suffered from distribution shifts when extrapolating unseen multi-hour trends.
  • Rule Reconstruction vs Disease Discovery: Air quality category classification learns categories defined by CPCB particulate concentration intervals. The classifier approximates the environmental standard rather than discovering an unobserved biological mechanism.
  • Epidemiological Scope: State-level annual respiratory disease rates cannot validate individual real-time campus health risk. Therefore, health advisories rely on transparent, rule-based medical vulnerability stratifications.
